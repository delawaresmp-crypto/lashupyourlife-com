The five minute morning

What getting ready looks like when your lashes are already done. Spoiler: you get your time back.

Think about how long your eyes take in the morning. Curl. Coat. Wait. Coat again. Fix the smudge. Then do it all in reverse at night. Lash extensions or a lash lift cut that whole routine out.

What your morning looks like with extensions

  1. Wash your face, keeping water pressure off your eyes.
  2. Brush your lashes out with a clean spoolie.
  3. Done. Go live your life.

That is it. No mascara, no curler, no raccoon eyes after the gym. You look put together on a no makeup day, and your full makeup days get faster too.

What your night looks like

Use an oil free cleanser around your eyes and skip heavy balms in that area. A few times a week, give your lashes a gentle clean with a lash safe foam. Sleep on your back if you can, or grab a silk pillowcase.

Who loves this the most

Parents getting kids out the door. Nurses and anyone on long shifts. People who work out before work. Anyone who travels a lot. If you are always short on time in the morning, this is for you.

Keeping it easy

The only real upkeep is a fill every two to three weeks. Put it on your calendar like a haircut and you never have to think about your lashes again. Kelsey can also do a lash lift and tint if you want something with even less upkeep.
